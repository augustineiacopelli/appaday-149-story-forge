DECISIONS (2026-10-03, from the Planning session, accepted as the plan of record):
  1. Story Forge writes only the story namespace plus sdq_.flag (Day 148 wrote only sdq_.giver). It never edits world.
  2. Gate flags and empty boss slots are done from the story side: story.bindings maps every Day 148 gate key to one flg_
     (optional display itm_ from rules); an empty boss slot gets an auto generated boss event starting a chosen trp_. Day
     150 resolves a boss as the world troop when set, else the story binding. dgn_.troop is never edited.
  3. Gate binding flags and scaffolded records get structural IDs (ENGINE_STORY.ids.structural); hand made ones use
     Kit.ids.mint.
  4. Conditions are JSON trees: all, any, not, flag (eq ne gt gte lt lte), item, chapter, quest (stage equals, stage
     reached, failed), true. No eval.
  5. Commands are a closed nested vocabulary with no goto: text, choice, if, setFlag, addFlag, giveItem, takeItem, gil,
     questStage, party, startBattle (win and lose lists), moveActor, face, wait, fade, music, sfx, changeMap, vehicle,
     callEvent, ending.
